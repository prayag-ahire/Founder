---
question: "Does angel tax still apply when I raise a seed round?"
tags: ["Funding", "Taxes & GST", "Pvt Limited"]
order: 9
---

**Angel tax** (the old Section 56(2)(viib) charge on share premiums above a “fair” value) has been **abolished** for shares issued from 1 April 2024. Resident and non-resident investments into Indian companies are no longer hit by that specific provision.

That said, the old tax was only one part of the fundraising paper trail. You still need a clean valuation for FEMA (if any non-resident is on the cap table), for income-tax documentation, and for board-level approval. Share-subscription agreements, PAS-3 filings, bank receipt trails, and proper issuance documents remain mandatory.

Treat “angel tax is gone” as “one famous headache is gone,” not “fundraising has no tax or compliance work.”
