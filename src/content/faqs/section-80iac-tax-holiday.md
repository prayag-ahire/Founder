A common misconception is that getting a DPIIT Startup India certificate automatically makes you tax-exempt. It does not.

To get a **100% deduction on company profits for 3 consecutive financial years** (out of the startup’s first 10 years), you must apply for a separate **Section 80-IAC certificate** approved by the **Inter-Ministerial Board (IMB)**.

Key criteria to qualify:
1. **Entity Type:** Must be incorporated as a Private Limited Company or LLP (incorporated between 1 April 2016 and 31 March 2025/extension).
2. **Turnover Limit:** Total turnover must not exceed ₹100 Crore in any financial year.
3. **Genuine Innovation:** The business must work towards innovation, development, or commercialisation of new products, processes, or services with significant potential for employment generation or wealth creation.
4. **Application Process:** Apply online via the Startup India portal with your DPIIT number, video pitch, pitch deck, audited financial statements, and patent/IP filings (if available).

**Reality check for founders:** The Inter-Ministerial Board rejects 95%+ of applications due to lack of deep technological or novel defensibility. Do not count on 80-IAC tax exemption for early-stage survival — treat it as a cherry on top if your business becomes profitable in year 3 or 4.
