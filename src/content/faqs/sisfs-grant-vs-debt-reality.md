A frequent shock for founders applying for the **Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS)** is expecting an unconditional ₹20 Lakh grant, only to receive a term sheet offering **Compulsorily Convertible Debentures (CCDs)** or debt with tough conditions.

Here is how the scheme actually works and what founders report from the ground:

### 1. The Two-Tier Structure of SISFS
Under the official Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) guidelines, SISFS provides two separate funding components:
- **Up to ₹20 Lakhs as a 100% Grant:** Exclusively intended for proof of concept (PoC), prototype development, or product trials.
- **Up to ₹50 Lakhs as Debt / CCDs:** For market entry, commercialisation, or scaling once you already have a working MVP or prototype.

### 2. Why Incubators Pivot to Debt or CCDs
The central government disburses funds to **registered incubators** (e.g. IIT/IIM incubation cells, T-Hub, etc.), and their internal **Incubator Seed Management Committee (ISMC)** holds full autonomy over whether to issue a grant or convertible debt:
- **High Competition:** Incubators receive hundreds of applications for a fixed pool of seed capital. If your startup is building generic SaaS or a marketplace with no physical hardware/deep-tech R&D costs, committees often reject grant requests and offer debt instruments instead.
- **Fund Recycling:** Repayments from debt or convertible debentures allow incubators to replenish their seed fund basket and back future batches.

### 3. Red Flags in SISFS Incubator Term Sheets
While government seed capital is genuinely non-dilutive or low-interest (typically 3–5-year repayment moratorium), founders should watch out for overreaching clauses:
- **Personal Guarantees / Promoter Liability:** Reject any clause that attempts to attach your personal residence, bank accounts, or promoter assets to a business failure. SISFS is venture-style seed support, not an aggressive collateralized bank loan.
- **Milestone Disbursement Bottlenecks:** SISFS money is never wired as a single lump-sum. It is disbursed in **3 to 4 tranches against strict milestone verification**. Delays of 3 to 6 months between committee selection and the first bank wire are common.

*Pro-Tip:* Apply to **up to 3 incubators simultaneously** on the Startup India portal to diversify your chances, and reach out to incubator managers before submitting to check if their current sectoral basket has active capital.
