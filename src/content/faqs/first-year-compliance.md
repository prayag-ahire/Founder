question: "What compliances does a new Private Limited have in the first year?"

A short first-year list that catches most new companies:

- **Board meetings** — at least four in a calendar year, with a gap of not more than 120 days (small-company relaxations may apply; confirm with your CS).
- **First auditor** appointment and statutory audit.
- **AOC-4** and **MGT-7/7A** with the ROC after the AGM.
- **Income tax** return and, if applicable, tax audit.
- **GST**, **TDS**, **PF/ESI** if you have crossed those thresholds.
- **Share certificates**, register of members, and deposit of subscription money.
- **Beneficial ownership** (SBO) and DIR-3 KYC for directors.

Put reminders on a calendar the week you incorporate. Late ROC filings are cheap compared with compounding later, but they look sloppy to investors.
