Beyond the Startup India Seed Fund, the Government of India operates specialized equity-free grant programs designed for deep-tech, hardware, biotech, and defense innovations. Founders refer to these programs as the **"Non-Dilutive Grant Ladder."**

Here are the 3 flagship grant schemes worth targeting:

### 1. DST NIDHI-PRAYAS (Department of Science & Technology)
- **Grant Amount:** Up to **₹10 Lakhs** (100% equity-free prototyping grant).
- **Best For:** Hardware, IoT, cleantech, robotics, and physical device prototyping.
- **How It Works:** Administered through designated **PRAYAS Centres (PCs)** hosted at engineering institutes (like IITs, NITs, and top research hubs). The funding pays for component sourcing, 3D printing, CNC machining, lab testing, and technical fabrication.
- **Key Advantage:** You do not even need an incorporated company to apply; individual innovators and student founders can apply directly through a host PRAYAS incubator.

### 2. BIRAC BIG (Biotechnology Ignition Grant)
- **Grant Amount:** Up to **₹50 Lakhs** for an 18-month project duration.
- **Best For:** Healthtech, medtech, diagnostics, agricultural biotechnology, and industrial bio-solutions.
- **How It Works:** Managed by the Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council (BIRAC) under the Department of Biotechnology. Applications open **twice a year** (typically January/February and July/August) through designated "BIG Partners" (such as FITT IIT Delhi, Venture Center Pune, C-CAMP Bengaluru, IKP Hyderabad).
- **Evaluation:** Highly academic and rigorous. Evaluated by specialized scientific review committees on novel intellectual property, technical feasibility, and clinical/commercial viability.

### 3. iDEX (Innovations for Defence Excellence)
- **Grant Amount:** Up to **₹1.5 Crores** (under the DISC / SPARK challenges).
- **Best For:** Aerospace, defense technology, drone systems, cybersecurity, satellite tech, and maritime solutions.
- **How It Works:** Ministry of Defence presents specific operational problem statements from the Indian Army, Navy, or Air Force. Winning startups receive non-dilutive milestone-based grants and direct fast-track procurement pathways.

### The Reality of Government Grants:
1. **Reimbursement & Tranche-Based:** Grants are never deposited overnight. They are released in 3–4 milestone tranches, and you must maintain immaculate utilization certificates (UCs) and Chartered Accountant audited expense statements.
2. **Timelines:** Budget **5 to 8 months** from the date of application submission to the first tranche arriving in your bank account.
