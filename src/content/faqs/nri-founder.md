---
question: "Can an NRI or foreigner be a founder or director of an Indian startup?"
tags: ["Pvt Limited", "FDI", "Setup"]
order: 16
---

**Yes, with conditions.** A Private Limited company needs at least one director who is a **resident in India** (182+ days in the previous calendar year). An NRI can be a co-founder, shareholder, and additional director. A company with only foreign directors will not sail through MCA.

Shareholding by NRIs and foreign nationals is **FDI**. Most software and product startups fall under the automatic route at 100%, but you must follow FEMA pricing, reporting (including FC-GPR after allotment), and sector caps. Some sectors need approval.

KYC is heavier: passport, overseas address proof, and notarised / apostilled documents are common. DIN and DSC still apply. Talk to a CA who does FEMA *before* you allot shares — fixing a wrong allotment is slower than incorporating correctly.
