---
question: "What is the Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS)?"
tags: ["Gov Schemes", "Funding"]
order: 6
---

**SISFS** gives early-stage money through government-selected incubators — not directly from a central portal to your bank account. Typical support is up to ₹20 lakh for proof of concept, prototype, or product trials, and up to ₹50 lakh for market entry, commercialisation, or scaling, depending on the incubator’s mandate.

You apply to a participating incubator. They diligence the idea, then disburse in tranches against milestones. DPIIT recognition and a real prototype (or a tight plan for one) help. This is grant-style / incubator funding, not a substitute for a priced VC round.

Check the current incubator list on the [Startup India](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/) site — the roster changes, and each incubator runs its own application window.
