question: "What are the mandatory compliances for a One Person Company (OPC)?"

A **One Person Company (OPC)** gives a solo founder the benefits of limited liability without needing a second director or shareholder. However, contrary to popular belief, **an OPC is still a corporate entity under the Companies Act 2013 and carries serious compliance requirements**.

Here is the full compliance checklist for an OPC:

### 1. Mandatory Statutory Audit by a CA (Every Year)
Unlike an LLP (which is exempt below ₹40L turnover), **an OPC MUST have its books of accounts audited every single financial year by an independent Chartered Accountant**, even if you had zero revenue and zero transactions!
- First auditor must be appointed via **Form ADT-1** within 30 days of incorporation.

### 2. Form AOC-4 (Financial Statements) — Due within 180 days of FY close (by 27 September)
- Contains balance sheet, profit and loss statement, auditor's report, and director's report.
- **Relief for OPCs:** An OPC is exempt from preparing a **Cash Flow Statement**.
- Director's report can be signed by the sole director alone.

### 3. Form MGT-7A (Abridged Annual Return) — Due within 60 days of submitting AOC-4 (by 26 November)
- OPCs and small companies file the simplified **Form MGT-7A** instead of the extensive MGT-7 required for larger corporations.
- Can be signed by the sole director without requiring certification by a practicing Company Secretary (CS).

### 4. Board Meetings
- An OPC with a single director is **exempt from holding board meetings**.
- If the OPC has more than one director (up to 15 allowed), at least **one board meeting must be held in each half of the calendar year**, with the gap between the two meetings being at least 90 days.
- OPCs are entirely exempt from holding an Annual General Meeting (AGM).

### 5. Annual Director KYC (DIR-3 KYC) — Due by 30 September
- The sole director must file annual KYC (DIR-3 KYC Web or eForm) on the MCA portal before 30th September. Missing it deactivates your DIN with a ₹5,000 late fee.

### 6. Nominee Updates (Form INC-4)
- An OPC must always have a registered Nominee Director. If the nominee withdraws consent or changes, the founder must file **Form INC-4** within 30 days.
