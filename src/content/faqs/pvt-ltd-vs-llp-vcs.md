---
question: "Why do Indian VCs prefer a Private Limited company over an LLP?"
tags: ["Pvt Limited", "LLP", "Equity & ESOP", "Funding"]
order: 1
---

Most Indian VCs will only write a cheque into a **Private Limited company**. The Companies Act, 2013 lets you issue equity, preference shares, and ESOPs with clean cap-table records. An LLP cannot issue shares, so there is no standard way to take preferred stock, run an ESOP pool, or do a priced round.

LLPs are fine for services firms and bootstrapped businesses. The moment you want institutional capital, convert (or incorporate) as a Pvt Ltd. Investors also prefer the governance, annual filings, and limited-liability structure that companies already have.

If you are still exploring, start with the entity you can actually operate — then convert before a priced round, not after term sheets are on the table.
