Use this as a default map, not legal advice:

- **Sole prop / Partnership** — testing a service, no outside capital, you want minimum cost.
- **LLP** — two or more partners, professional firm, limited liability, no plan to issue ESOPs or raise VC.
- **OPC** — one founder, want a company and limited liability, no co-founder or investor yet.
- **Pvt Limited** — you will hire, offer ESOPs, raise angels/VCs, or build a product company.

A Pvt Ltd needs at least **two directors** and can start with two shareholders (they can be the same people). Compliance is heavier (board meetings, auditor, ROC). That cost is usually worth it if a priced round is even a 12-month possibility.

You can convert later. Conversion takes months and professional fees — cheaper than rebuilding a cap table after a messy LLP raise.
