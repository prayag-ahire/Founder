A **Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)** has significantly lower maintenance overhead than a Private Limited Company (no mandatory statutory audit if below threshold, no formal board meetings or AGM). However, the ROC filings it *does* require carry severe penalties if missed.

Here is the complete annual compliance calendar for an LLP:

### 1. LLP Form 11 (Annual Return) — Due: 30 May
- **What it is:** A summary of designated partners, management affairs, and partner contributions.
- **Deadline:** Within 60 days of the financial year close (i.e. **30th May** every year).
- **Penalty for delay:** **₹100 per day** with no upper ceiling. A delay of 6 months can cost over ₹18,000 in penalties alone.

### 2. LLP Form 8 (Statement of Account & Solvency) — Due: 30 October
- **What it is:** The financial statement declaring assets, liabilities, income, and whether the LLP is solvent (able to pay debts).
- **Deadline:** Within 30 days from 6 months of FY end (i.e. **30th October**).
- **Audit Requirement:**
  - **No audit needed** if turnover is below **₹40 Lakhs** AND partner capital contribution is below **₹25 Lakhs**. Partners can self-certify.
  - If turnover exceeds ₹40 Lakhs OR capital exceeds ₹25 Lakhs, accounts **must be audited** by a practicing Chartered Accountant.
- **Penalty for delay:** **₹100 per day** indefinitely until filed.

### 3. Income Tax Return (ITR-5) — Due: 31 July / 31 October
- LLPs are taxed at a flat rate of **30%** plus applicable surcharge and cess.
- Due **31 July** if accounts are not subject to audit.
- Due **31 October** if tax audit / LLP Act statutory audit applies.

### 4. LLP Form 3 (Initial Filing - Within 30 Days of Incorporation)
- While not an annual filing, you must draft and execute your **LLP Agreement** on state-specific non-judicial stamp paper and file **Form 3** within 30 days of registration.
- Many new founders forget this: missing Form 3 incurs the same brutal **₹100/day** penalty from day 31 onwards!
