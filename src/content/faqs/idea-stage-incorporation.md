**Do not incorporate on day one just because you came up with a startup idea.**

Once you register a Private Limited company or LLP, mandatory statutory clocks start ticking immediately under Indian corporate law — regardless of whether you have made ₹0 or ₹10 Lakhs:
- Annual ROC filings (AOC-4, MGT-7) with mandatory Chartered Accountant audits.
- DIR-3 KYC for all directors every single year.
- Income tax filings, board meetings, and potential bank penalty fees if minimum balance isn't maintained.
- Closing an unused company (strike-off under Section 248) takes 6–12 months and ₹15,000–₹25,000 in professional fees.

**When you SHOULD incorporate:**
1. **Closing Outside Capital:** An angel investor, syndicate, or VC fund is ready to wire funds or issue a term sheet (cheques can only be accepted into a corporate account).
2. **Customer Contracts & Invoicing:** Enterprise clients or B2B pilots demand a GST invoice and vendor onboarding agreement.
3. **Payment Gateways & Regulated APIs:** Integrating gateways like Razorpay, Cashfree, or financial APIs that require a corporate entity and current account.
4. **Co-founder IP Assignment:** Multiple co-founders contributing code or patents full-time, and you need formal intellectual property assignment to avoid future disputes.

Before that point, talk to 50 potential users, build a prototype or landing page, and validate demand.
