Across founder communities, over 60–70% of first-time **DPIIT Startup India** recognition applications get rejected or sent back for clarification. The rejection is almost never about your incorporation documents — it is about how you describe your innovation and scalability.

Here are the top reasons for rejection on the National Single Window System (NSWS) portal and how to fix them:

### 1. The "Marketing Fluff" Trap
- **The Mistake:** Writing generic buzzwords like *"We are an AI-driven ecosystem revolutionizing the market"* or copy-pasting from a pitch deck or marketing brochure.
- **What Reviewers Look For:** Specific technical or operational differentiation. Explain the exact mechanism: *What is the technological novelty? How does your workflow reduce cost, increase efficiency, or solve a localized Indian bottleneck compared to existing players?*

### 2. Failure to Show "Scalability"
Under the Startup India definition, an entity must have high potential for employment generation or wealth creation.
- If your business model looks like a standard digital marketing agency, IT staffing consultancy, or traditional trader/reseller, it will be flagged as an unscalable services business.
- **The Fix:** Clearly demonstrate non-linear scalability — how software, proprietary IP, or automation allows your user base or revenue to grow 10x without hiring 10x headcount.

### 3. Discrepancies with MCA / Registrar Records
- Even minor mismatches between your NSWS application and MCA/LLP records trigger automated or manual rejections:
  - Registered office address differing from incorporation certificates.
  - Object clause in your **MOA (Memorandum of Association)** not matching the activities described in your application.
  - Applying for an entity formed by splitting or reconstructing an existing business (strictly prohibited).

### 4. What To Do If Your Application is Rejected
- **You cannot edit a rejected application:** Once rejected, the portal locks that submission. You must submit a fresh application.
- **Cooling-Off Window:** Depending on the portal cycle, you may need to wait out a 1-to-3-month cooling-off period before the system accepts a fresh filing with the same CIN/LLPIN.
- **Rewrite the technical brief:** Do not re-submit the same text. Break down your answer into 3 clean parts: **1) The specific problem, 2) The proprietary/technical solution, 3) The commercialization & unit economic scalability.**
