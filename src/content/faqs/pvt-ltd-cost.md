---
question: "How much does it cost to register a Private Limited company in India?"
tags: ["Pvt Limited", "Setup"]
order: 15
---

Government fees depend on authorised capital and state (stamp duty on MOA/AOA). For a simple two-director company with modest capital, **MCA + stamp duty** is often in the low thousands of rupees, not lakhs.

What founders actually pay is **professional fees** on top: CA or company-secretary charges for SPICe+, DSC, PAN/TAN, and a registered-office package. A straightforward incorporation commonly lands in a **₹5,000–₹15,000** band from a good firm; rush jobs, foreign directors, or trademark work cost more.

After incorporation, budget the **first-year run-rate**: auditor, ROC filings, bookkeeping, and GST if you register. That recurring cost is why people start as a sole prop when they are only testing. If you know you will raise or hire, pay for the Pvt Ltd once and do it cleanly.
