Once your Certificate of Incorporation arrives from the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), the clock starts on several mandatory e-forms on the **MCA V3 portal**. Missing them leads to heavy fines or corporate standstill.

Here is the essential MCA form master list for your company's first year:

| Form | Name & Purpose | Deadline | Consequences if Missed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **INC-20A** | **Declaration for Commencement of Business**<br>Directors certify that subscribers deposited their initial share capital into the company bank account (with bank statement proof). | Within **180 days** of incorporation | Company **cannot commence operations or borrow money**. ROC can initiate strike-off. Penalty of ₹50,000 on company + ₹1,000/day on directors. |
| **ADT-1** | **Appointment of Statutory Auditor**<br>Appointing the independent CA firm who will audit financial statements. | Within **15 days** of the first Board meeting (or within 30 days of incorporation). | Normal ROC late fees; accounts cannot be audited or filed. |
| **DIR-3 KYC** | **Director KYC Verification**<br>Verifying mobile, email, and Aadhaar/passport details of all directors holding a DIN. | By **30 September** each year. | DIN gets deactivated; cannot sign any MCA forms. **₹5,000 penalty** per DIN to reactivate. |
| **DPT-3** | **Return of Deposits / Loans**<br>Disclosing all loans, advances, or money taken from directors, relatives, or banks. | By **30 June** for the preceding financial year. | Serious penalties under Section 73/76 of Companies Act (₹1 Lakh to ₹10 Lakhs). |
| **AOC-4** | **Filing of Financial Statements**<br>Balance sheet, P&L, notes, and CA audit report. | Within **30 days** of AGM (OPC: within 180 days of FY close). | **₹100 per day** penalty until filed; potential director disqualification after 3 consecutive years. |
| **MGT-7 / 7A** | **Annual Return**<br>Shareholding pattern, transfers, director remuneration, and meeting records. | Within **60 days** of AGM (OPC: Form MGT-7A). | **₹100 per day** penalty indefinitely. |
| **PAS-3** | **Return of Allotment of Shares**<br>Filed whenever you issue shares to founders, angels, or investors. | Within **30 days** of share allotment. | Penalty of ₹1,000/day (up to ₹10 Lakhs) on the company and defaulting officers. |
| **MGT-14** | **Filing of Special Resolutions**<br>Filed when altering MOA/AOA, creating ESOP pools, or taking shareholder approval for capital changes. | Within **30 days** of passing the resolution. | Standard MCA late fees and inability to enforce amended clauses. |

All filings must be signed using the directors' Class 3 Digital Signature Certificates (DSC) and submitted through your registered profile on the MCA V3 portal.
