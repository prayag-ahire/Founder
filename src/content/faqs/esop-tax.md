---
question: "How are ESOPs taxed for employees of Indian startups?"
tags: ["Equity & ESOP", "Taxes & GST", "Pvt Limited"]
order: 4
---

ESOPs are usually taxed at **two points**:

1. **Exercise** — the gap between fair market value and the exercise price is taxed as a perquisite under salary.
2. **Sale** — any further gain is capital gains (holding period starts from the exercise date).

For eligible DPIIT-recognised startups, employees may **defer the perquisite tax** subject to the statutory conditions and the applicable timeline; this is not automatic in every case. The company still has to report this correctly in payroll and withhold when the deferral ends.

Grant and vesting themselves are generally not taxable. Get a valuation and a written ESOP plan before you promise options — informal “you’ll get 1%” emails create disputes and tax mess later.
