question: "How do I reserve a name and incorporate a Private Limited company?"

The usual MCA path looks like this:

1. Get **DSC** (digital signature) for directors and a **DIN** if they do not already have one.
2. Run a name check and file **SPICe+ Part A** (name reservation). Keep a few backups; trademark conflicts get rejected.
3. File **SPICe+ Part B** with MOA, AOA, registered office proof, and identity/address KYC. PAN, TAN, EPFO, ESIC, and optional GST/bank/EPFO can be bundled.
4. Receive the **Certificate of Incorporation**. Open a current account, issue share certificates, and deposit share capital.

Most first-time founders use a CA or company-secretary firm. Budget professional fees plus government fees, and keep the registered office address real — virtual offices are common but must satisfy MCA documentation.

After incorporation: first board meeting, appointment of auditor, and statutory registers. Skipping those is how “we incorporated last year” turns into a due-diligence failure.
