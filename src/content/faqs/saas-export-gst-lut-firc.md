If you sell SaaS, software development, or digital services to clients outside India (via Stripe, Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, or direct wire), your revenue qualifies as an **"Export of Services"** under Section 16 of the IGST Act, which is classified as a **Zero-Rated Supply**.

However, to legally sell at 0% GST without getting slapped with an 18% tax demand during an audit, you must establish a strict compliance trail:

### 1. File a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) Every Financial Year
- **What it is:** Form **GST RFD-11 (LUT)** submitted online via the GST portal (`gst.gov.in`).
- It binds your company to fulfill all export conditions and allows you to invoice foreign customers at **0% IGST**.
- **Crucial Rule:** You must file the LUT at the **start of each financial year (in March/April)** *before* raising export invoices. Filing it late risks tax liabilities.

### 2. The 4 Legal Conditions for Zero-Rated Export
Under Section 2(6) of the IGST Act, ALL four conditions must be met:
1. The supplier of service is located in India.
2. The recipient of service is located outside India.
3. The place of supply of service is outside India (the customer uses the software overseas).
4. **Payment is received in convertible foreign exchange** (USD, EUR, GBP, etc.) or in Indian Rupees through authorized banking channels backed by an official forex trail.

### 3. FIRC / FIRA (Foreign Inward Remittance Advice) & RBI Purpose Codes
To satisfy GST auditors and the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), you need bank-certified proof that your income originated abroad:
- **RBI Purpose Code:** Instruct your bank or payment processor to map your incoming wires to the correct software purpose code — typically **P0802** (Software consultancy/implementation) or **P0807** (Off-site software development).
- **FIRA / Inward Advice:** Issued by your Authorized Dealer (AD) bank (e.g. HDFC, ICICI) whenever foreign funds hit your account. Keep every FIRA filed alongside its corresponding invoice.
- **The Merchant of Record (MoR) Warning:** If you use an MoR like Paddle or Lemon Squeezy, ensure their payouts do not land as a generic domestic NEFT/RTGS from an Indian intermediary without a FIRA. If your bank cannot issue a FIRA proving foreign inward remittance, your CA cannot defend the 0% GST claim under your LUT.
