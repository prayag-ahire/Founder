While central schemes like SISFS and NIDHI-PRAYAS get national headlines, individual state governments run some of the most generous **100% equity-free, non-dilutive grant programs** for early-stage founders.

Here are the prominent state-level startup grants you should explore based on where your company is registered:

### 1. Karnataka: ELEVATE 100 & ELEVATE NxT
- **Grant Amount:** Up to **₹50 Lakhs** (and up to **₹1 Crore** for deep-tech / ELEVATE NxT tracks).
- **Eligibility:** Startups registered in Karnataka with DPIIT recognition and an active MVP/prototype. Separate specialized tracks exist for women founders (ELEVATE Womenn), rural innovators (Beyond Bengaluru), and SC/ST entrepreneurs.
- **Process:** Multi-stage evaluation consisting of an online application, regional screening, and an in-person pitch before a jury of VC investors, industry veterans, and state bureaucrats.
- **Disbursement:** 100% grant disbursed in 2–3 milestone-based tranches against submitted bills and Chartered Accountant Utilisation Certificates (UCs).

### 2. Tamil Nadu: TANSEED (Tamil Nadu Startup Mission - TANSIM)
- **Grant Amount:** Up to **₹10 Lakhs to ₹15 Lakhs** equity-free seed grant.
- **Eligibility:** DPIIT-recognized startups incorporated in Tamil Nadu or with substantial operations/employment within the state.
- **Process:** Periodic cohort calls (TANSEED 6.0, 7.0, etc.) evaluated on technical novelty, local socio-economic impact, and commercial scalability.
- **Perk:** Winning TANSEED startups gain priority access to government incubation centers, state procurement opportunities, and co-working subsidies.

### 3. Gujarat: Startup Assistance Scheme
- **Benefits:**
  - **Founder Sustenance Allowance:** ₹20,000/month (₹25,000/month for women founders) for up to 1 year while building the product.
  - **Seed / Prototype Support:** Up to ₹30 Lakhs for prototype development and raw materials.
  - **Marketing Assistance:** Up to ₹15 Lakhs for commercialisation and market entry.
- **Eligibility:** Startups registered in Gujarat and incubated at recognized state nodal institutions (e.g. i-Hub, PDEU, Gujarat University).

### 4. Kerala: KSUM Seed Support
- **Benefits:** Up to **₹12 Lakhs** soft-loan / seed support through Kerala Startup Mission (KSUM), plus product trial and marketing grants.

### How State Grants Differ from Central Schemes
- **Zero Dilution:** Unlike angel syndicates, states take **0% equity** and zero board seats.
- **Local Footprint Required:** Your registered office, incorporation address, or primary team must be located in that state.
- **Bureaucratic Timeline:** Budget **4 to 7 months** between pitching and receiving your first bank wire, as state finance departments require thorough milestone validation.
