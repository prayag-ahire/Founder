Every director in a Private Limited/OPC or designated partner in an LLP needs two core credentials to interact with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA): a **DSC (Digital Signature Certificate)** and a **DIN (Director Identification Number)**.

### 1. Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)
- **What it is:** A secure cryptographic signature stored on a physical USB crypto-token (e.g. ePass 2003) issued by a Licensed Certifying Authority (e-Mudhra, Capricorn, Vsign).
- **Required Class:** You must obtain a **Class 3 DSC** with both signing and encryption capability.
- **Validity:** Typically issued for 2 or 3 years. When it expires, you must renew it and update it on the MCA portal.
- **How it works:** Whenever an MCA e-form or PDF document is generated, the founder plugs the USB token into their computer and enters a PIN to cryptographically sign the document. It carries the exact same legal weight as a wet ink signature under the Information Technology Act, 2000.

### 2. Director Identification Number (DIN / DPIN)
- **What it is:** An 8-digit unique identification number issued by the MCA to an individual intending to be a director of a company (called **DPIN** in an LLP).
- **How to obtain:** 
  - **New company:** Up to 3 DINs can be applied for simultaneously within the **SPICe+** incorporation form.
  - **Existing company:** If adding a director to an active company, file **Form DIR-3** with Aadhaar, PAN, and address proof certified by a CS/CA.
- **Lifetime validity:** Once issued, a DIN remains valid for life and is used across all company directorships.

### 3. Using DSC & DIN on the MCA V3 Portal
Under the upgraded MCA V3 portal:
1. **Create an "Associate" or "Business User" Login:** Register an account on `mca.gov.in` mapped to your PAN and DIN.
2. **Associate your DSC:** Go to *MCA Services → DSC Services → Associate DSC*. Plug in your USB token, launch the emBridge / MCA token utility, and associate your DSC with your DIN/PAN profile.
3. **Annual DIR-3 KYC:** Between 1 April and 30 September every year, every DIN holder must verify their KYC details. If phone and email remain identical, complete it in 2 minutes via **DIR-3 KYC Web** (free). If missed after September 30, a **₹5,000 fine** is levied to reactivate the DIN.
