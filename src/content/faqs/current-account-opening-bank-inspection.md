Opening a corporate current account for a newly incorporated Private Limited company or LLP is often surprisingly frustrating. Founders frequently report having their applications rejected or indefinitely stalled by banks like HDFC, ICICI, Kotak, Axis, or SBI.

The culprit is almost always the mandatory **"Physical Workplace Verification"** required under Reserve Bank of India (RBI) KYC Master Directions.

Here is why banks reject applications and how to ensure your startup gets approved on the first attempt:

### 1. Top Reasons for Rejection
1. **Home / Residential Office without Commercial Signage:**
   Under RBI anti-money laundering norms, the bank sends an outsourced third-party agency to inspect your Principal Place of Business (PPOB). If the agent sees a residential flat or bedroom with no sign that a business operates there, they file a "Non-Commercial / Negative" report.
2. **Virtual Offices with Restrictive Bank Policies:**
   While virtual offices are legal for MCA and GST registration, certain bank branches have internal risk blacklists for shared addresses where no dedicated physical desk exists during an unannounced inspection.
3. **Empty Plots or Under-Construction Sites:**
   Registering an unbuilt property or empty warehouse without an active operational desk causes automatic rejection.

### 2. How to Guarantee Approval During Physical Verification
- **Install a Proper Company Nameplate Outside:**
  Before the bank's verification agent arrives, install a clear, formal nameboard or acrylic signage on your office or apartment door displaying:
  - Full Legal Name (e.g. *Acme Technologies Private Limited*)
  - Corporate Identification Number (**CIN / LLPIN**)
  - Registered Office address
- **Prepare the Document Binder:**
  Keep physical, self-attested copies ready on-site:
  - Certificate of Incorporation (COI)
  - Company PAN card
  - Form INC-20A acknowledgement (if incorporated > 180 days)
  - Rent agreement + Landlord NOC + latest electricity bill (matching the registered office address)
  - Directors’ PAN and Aadhaar cards
  - Board Resolution authorizing the opening of the current account and designating authorized signatories.
- **Connect with a Startup Branch / Dedicated Relationship Manager (RM):**
  Avoid applying at small retail neighborhood branches that deal primarily with personal savings accounts and local shopkeepers. Open your account at a **flagship startup branch** or through the bank’s dedicated startup desk (e.g., *HDFC SmartHub Vyapar / Startup Program*, *ICICI iStartup*), where RMs understand tech entities and home-office models.
