In India, standard patent applications can take anywhere from **3 to 5 years** from filing to grant due to backlogs at the Indian Patent Office (IPO). For early-stage tech and hardware startups, waiting 5 years to protect proprietary intellectual property can kill fundraising or allow competitors to copy features.

Under the amended **Patent Rules, 2003** and the Startup Intellectual Property Protection (SIPP) scheme, DPIIT-recognized startups receive two massive statutory advantages: **expedited examination** and **massive fee reductions**.

Here is how to take advantage of the system:

### 1. Expedited Patent Examination (Form 18A)
- **Normal Process:** After filing a patent, it enters a standard queue. Examination reports take 24–36 months.
- **Startup Fast-Track Route:** As a DPIIT-recognized startup, you are eligible to file **Form 18A (Request for Expedited Examination)**.
- **The Result:** The Patent Examiner is legally mandated to issue the First Examination Report (FER) within **1 to 3 months**, and the final patent is frequently granted within **12 to 18 months** from filing.

### 2. Up to 80% Statutory Fee Reductions
Official government statutory filing fees are drastically slashed for recognized startups:
- **Patent E-filing (Form 1):** Slashed by **80%** — startups pay only **₹1,600** compared to the standard corporate fee of ₹8,000.
- **Request for Examination:** Drops from ₹20,000 to **₹4,000**.
- **Trademark E-filing (Single Class):** Slashed by **50%** — startups pay **₹4,500** per class compared to the standard corporate fee of ₹9,000.

### 3. Government-Funded IP Facilitators (SIPP Scheme)
Under the Startup Intellectual Property Protection (SIPP) scheme:
- The Controller General of Patents, Designs and Trademarks empanels official **IP Facilitators** (registered patent and trademark attorneys).
- **The Government Pays the Attorney Fees:** The legal drafting and advisory fees charged by the empanelled facilitators are paid directly by the central government. The startup only pays the subsidized statutory government filing fee.
- You can access the official list of empanelled IP facilitators directly on the [IP India portal](https://ipindia.gov.in/).

### 4. How to Claim the Benefit
When your patent attorney or CS files the online application on the `ipindia.gov.in` portal, select the applicant category as **"Startup"** and upload your **DPIIT Recognition Certificate (Form DIPP-xxxx)**.
