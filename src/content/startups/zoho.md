Zoho (Sridhar Vembu and team) is the long counter-example to “raise, grow, exit.” It is a private, profitable suite — CRM, mail, books, desk, and dozens of other apps — used by businesses worldwide, with a large engineering base in smaller Indian cities.

## Why founders study it

- Distribution came from product depth and word of mouth, not a Super Bowl ad.
- They treated rural / semi-urban talent as an advantage, not a compromise.
- Staying private meant they could build for decades without a quarterly narrative.

## What to steal

You do not have to copy the “no VC” religion. Copy the patience: pick a workflow companies pay for every month, and keep the product family coherent. Zoho’s lesson is that an Indian SaaS company can be global without looking like a Bay Area clone.
