Lalit Keshre, Harsh Jain, Ishan Bansal, and Neeraj Singh started Groww to make mutual funds less intimidating. The app later added stocks and more of the wealth stack. Design and plain language were the growth engine at a time when most brokers still looked like 2009.

## Why founders study it

- They picked a moment: smartphone-first investors who did not want a relationship manager.
- Trust was built with UI calmness and education, then expanded into adjacent products.
- The company is a reminder that consumer fintech still has to live with SEBI, exchanges, and custody — onboarding friction is part of the product.

## What to steal

If your user is new to a regulated category, reduce shame and jargon before you add more features. Groww’s early advantage was making a first SIP feel obvious.
